## The question

Onion water is red-brown. Is that one colour molecule, or a yellow and a red and a brown all together? Tea is brown, but brown isn't in the rainbow. What is it made of?

## Theory: chromatography (15 minutes)

Read **Chromatography**. You'll learn:

- why a moving solvent carries some molecules further than others,
- why you draw the start line in **pencil**, and keep the solvent below it,
- how to calculate an Rf value, $R_f = \dfrac{\text{distance moved by the spot}}{\text{distance moved by the solvent}}$.

## Do: run the strips (35 minutes)

1. Cut strips of white coffee filter 2 cm wide and 12 cm long. Handle them by the top edge only.
2. Draw a pencil line 2 cm from the bottom. Write the extract's name at the top in pencil.
3. With a toothpick or the tip of a match stick, put a small dot of extract on the line. Let it dry (blow on it). Add another dot on the same place. Repeat **five times** to make a strong, small spot.
4. Put 1 cm of solvent in a clear cup: water with a pinch of salt.
5. Hang the strip from a pencil laid across the cup, with the bottom just in the solvent and the pencil line **above** it.
6. Wait until the solvent is about 1 cm from the top (10–20 minutes). Take the strip out and mark the solvent front in pencil straight away.

Run every extract, plus a **known** single colour on its own strip if the guide gives you one (a food colouring).

## Notice something puzzling

The karkade and cabbage spots move a long way. The turmeric spot hardly moves at all. Did the turmeric fail, or is it telling you something about turmeric?

## Do it better: change the solvent (15 minutes)

**No stove or flame may be lit in the room for this part.** Rubbing alcohol (70% alcohol from a pharmacy) is flammable.

Run a turmeric strip and an onion strip again, with rubbing alcohol as the solvent instead of salt water. Put a lid or a plate over the cup so the alcohol doesn't evaporate. Compare.

## Record (10 minutes)

For each strip, measure from the pencil line to the **centre** of each spot, and to the solvent front, in centimetres.

| Extract | Solvent | Solvent front (cm) | Spot colour | Spot distance (cm) | $R_f$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

Stick the dried strips in your notebook. Answer:

1. Which extracts are mixtures of more than one colour? How do you know?
2. Why did turmeric move in alcohol but not in water? Use the word *dissolve*.
3. A spot has $R_f = 0.65$ and the solvent front is at 8.0 cm. Where is the spot?
