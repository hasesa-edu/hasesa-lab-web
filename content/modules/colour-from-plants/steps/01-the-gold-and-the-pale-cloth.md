## The dye-room rules first (10 minutes)

Before any pot is heated, your guide reads out the rules. Copy them into the front of your notebook:

1. Goggles on whenever ash water, alum, vinegar or baking soda is on the table.
2. Gloves on whenever your hands go near a dye bath or mordant.
3. Only the guide lights the stove and lifts hot pots and jars. Stay an arm's length away.
4. Nothing is eaten or drunk, even the karkade and the tea.
5. Ash water in an eye: rinse with running water for 15 minutes and shout for the guide.

## Look closely (10 minutes)

Pass round a netela, a gabi or a kemis with a tibeb border. Look at the coloured threads with a magnifier if you have one.

- How many different colours can you count?
- Is each thread one colour all through, or only coloured on the outside? (Gently untwist a loose end if the guide allows.)
- Which colours look faded? Where on the cloth?

Shemane (weavers) today mostly buy thread already dyed in factories. For most of history, every one of those colours came from plants, insects or minerals, boiled up by hand.

## Watch (10 minutes)

Your guide stands a glass jar of hot water in a pot on the stove, drops in a handful of onion skins and stirs. Watch the water change colour.

Then two pieces of white cotton go in, marked **A** and **B**. They look the same, but the guide soaked one of them in something last night. After 15 minutes the guide lifts both out with tongs and rinses them under the tap.

Then the guide washes both with soap and rubs hard for a minute.

## Predict (10 minutes)

On a page titled *The mystery*, write:

1. **Why did the onion skins colour the water?** Where was the colour hiding?
2. **Why did A and B come out different?** Write your best guess, then a second one.
3. **Guess:** which of these will dye cotton best, and which worst: onion skins, turmeric (ird), karkade, red cabbage, coffee grounds, tea? Put them in order.

## Questions (5 minutes)

As a team, list every question you have. For example:

- Why are onion skins orange when onions are white?
- Would hotter water give more colour?
- Why does tea stain a cup but karkade doesn't seem to?
- Why does the sun fade a gabi left on the fence?
