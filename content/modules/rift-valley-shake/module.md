Earthquakes don't kill people; collapsing buildings do. In ten hours you'll find out why Ethiopia shakes, build your own earthquake machine, and use it to discover what keeps a building standing. Then you'll design a tower to beat it.

1. **The shaking table** (45 min). Two towers, one survives. Why?
2. **A cracking continent** (75 min). Model the Rift and send P and S waves along a slinky.
3. **Build a shake table** (90 min). Make an earthquake you can control and measure.
4. **Strong shapes** (75 min). Squares, triangles, tension and compression.
5. **Sway and resonance** (90 min). Every tower has its own rhythm. Find it.
6. **Design and test** (120 min). Build your tower to a budget, test it, improve it.
7. **The big shake** (60 min). The final test, and your explanation.

By the end you'll understand plate tectonics, seismic waves, frequency, resonance, forces in structures and how engineers make buildings safer.
