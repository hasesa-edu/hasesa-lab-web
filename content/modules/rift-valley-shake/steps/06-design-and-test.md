## The challenge

Build a tower that:

- is at least **50 cm tall**,
- carries a **200 g** load on a platform at the top,
- survives 20 seconds at **each** of test levels 1, 2 and 3 on the shake table,
- uses no more than the budget: **100 spaghetti sticks, 2 m of tape, 100 g of clay, 2 m of string**, and anything else from the materials table that the guide approves.

Its base may be fixed to a 20 × 20 cm card, which is taped to the shake table.

## Design (25 minutes)

Using your rules from steps 4 and 5, sketch at least two designs. For each, label:

- where the bracing goes,
- how wide the base is,
- where the heavy parts are,
- any damping or isolation.

Choose one. Write **why** you chose it.

## Build (50 minutes)

Build carefully. Most towers fail at their **joints**: make every joint strong and neat.

## Test and improve (35 minutes)

1. Test at level 1 for 20 seconds. Film it in slow motion if you can.
2. If it survives, go to level 2, then 3.
3. If it fails, find **exactly where** it failed first (watch the video). Fix that part only, then test again.

Record every test: level, survived or not, where it failed, what you changed.

## Theory

Re-read **How engineers fight back** in *Structures and forces*. Which of those ideas did your tower use?

## Record (10 minutes)

A labelled drawing of your final tower, your test log, and a sentence for each design choice with the evidence that supports it.
