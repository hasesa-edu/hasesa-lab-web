## The test (30 minutes)

Every team's tower is measured (at least 50 cm?), checked against the budget, and loaded with 200 g. Then the guide runs the class shake table, the same way for every tower: level 1, level 2, level 3, 20 seconds each. Glasses on.

The class records, for each tower: height, mass of materials used, highest level survived, and where it failed (if it did).

## Back to the mystery (10 minutes)

Open *The mystery* from step 1. Next to each guess, write ✓ or ✗ and what you now know. Then answer the first question properly: **what made the difference between the tower that fell and the one that survived?** Use the words *bracing, triangle, natural frequency, resonance*.

## Explain it to the guide (20 minutes)

Your guide will ask each of you to:

- explain why the Rift Valley has earthquakes,
- show how the gap between P and S waves gives the distance to an earthquake,
- explain your frame test and why triangles are stiff,
- explain your resonance graph,
- explain each design choice in your tower, with evidence,
- say what you'd change to make it even better.

Talk at home about one thing your family could do to be safer in an earthquake (for example: where to shelter, securing heavy shelves).
