## Watch

Your guide has a shaking table with two towers on it, each about 50 cm tall, made of the same spaghetti, tape and clay, with the same weight on top. Look at them closely first. Draw both.

Glasses on. The guide shakes the table, slowly at first, then faster. Watch both towers carefully:

- Which one moves more? Where does it bend?
- When does the first one fail? Where does it break first?
- Does the survivor move with the table, or does its top lag behind?

The guide repeats the test with two new towers, if there's time.

## Predict

On a page titled *The mystery*, write:

1. **Why did one tower survive?** Point to the differences in your drawings.
2. **Do you think a taller tower is always weaker?** Why?
3. **What do you think causes earthquakes in Ethiopia?**
4. **Has anyone in your family felt an earthquake?** Ask at home: when, where, and what did it feel like?

## Look at the map

Your guide shows a map of the East African Rift. Find Addis Ababa, Adama, the Awash River, the Afar and the Rift Valley lakes. Mark where earthquakes have happened.

## Questions

As a team, list every question you have. For example:

- Why is Africa splitting apart, and how fast?
- Could a big earthquake happen in Addis?
- Why do some buildings fall and others not?
- How do scientists measure earthquakes?

## The first clue

Read *Earthquakes and the Rift Valley* tonight. Next time, you'll make a continent crack.
