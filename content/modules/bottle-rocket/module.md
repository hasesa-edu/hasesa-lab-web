A bottle shoots into the sky with nothing inside but water and squeezed air. In eight hours you find out why, build your own rocket, and run a real experiment to make it fly as high as possible.

1. **The mystery** (45 min). Watch the guide launch a rocket. Predict, ask questions, and learn the launch rules.
2. **Push and push back** (75 min). Race balloon rockets along a string and discover Newton's laws.
3. **Squeezed air** (60 min). Feel pressure with syringes and bottles, and calculate the force on a rocket's nozzle.
4. **Build your rocket** (90 min). Fins, nose cone and balance. First test launches.
5. **How high?** (75 min). Build a clinometer and measure heights you can't reach with trigonometry.
6. **The water question** (90 min). A fair test: how much water makes the rocket fly highest?
7. **Launch day** (60 min). Final launches, your graph, and your explanation.

By the end you'll understand forces, Newton's laws, pressure, the tangent ratio and how to design a fair test, and you'll have the numbers to prove what makes your rocket fly.
