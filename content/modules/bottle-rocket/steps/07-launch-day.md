## Final launches (25 minutes)

Each team gets two launches with their best rocket and their best amount of water, at the class's agreed pressure. Two other teams measure the height with their clinometers. The class records every result on a big board.

## Back to the mystery (15 minutes)

Open the page titled *The mystery* from step 1. Next to each guess, write ✓ or ✗ and what you now know. How close was your height guess?

Answer the first question properly, in your own words: **what pushed the rocket up?** Use these words: *squeezed air, pressure, force, water, Newton's third law, weight*.

## Explain it to the guide (20 minutes)

Your guide will ask each of you about your rocket and your results. Be ready to:

- explain why the rocket goes up, and why it stops going up,
- show how you measured a height, with the working,
- show your fair-test table and graph, and say which amount of water was best,
- explain why too much water and too little water both fly badly,
- say what you would change next to fly even higher, and what fair test would check it.

Take your rocket home. It's yours.
