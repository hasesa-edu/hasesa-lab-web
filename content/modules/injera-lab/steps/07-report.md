## The whole story (30 minutes)

Make a flowchart, on a large sheet, of injera from flour to eyes. It must include:

- teff flour, water and ersho,
- **enzymes** (amylase) turning starch into sugar,
- **yeast** fermenting sugar into carbon dioxide and ethanol, with the word equation,
- **lactic acid bacteria** fermenting sugar into lactic acid, with the word equation,
- the pH falling, with numbers from your step 4 results,
- what temperature does to the speed, with your step 3 graph,
- the mitad: how the eyes form.

## Back to the mystery (15 minutes)

Open *The mystery* from step 1. Mark each guess ✓ or ✗ and add what you now know. Then answer the first question properly: **who is doing all that work in the dark?**

## Present (30 minutes)

Each team presents its baking experiment in three minutes: what you changed, what you kept the same, what you measured, and what you found.

Then your guide will talk with each of you. Be ready to:

- explain how the balloon and gas tests show that living things make the bubbles,
- show your rate–temperature graph and explain its shape,
- show your pH results and explain them,
- explain what amylase does and why boiled malt didn't work,
- write both fermentation word equations,
- say what you'd tell a family whose injera has few eyes, using evidence.
