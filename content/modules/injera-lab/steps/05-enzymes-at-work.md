## The puzzle

Yeast eats **sugar**. But teff flour is mostly **starch**, not sugar. So how do the yeast and bacteria in batter get their food?

## Do: the malt test (40 minutes)

Your guide has made two liquids:

- **Starch solution**: a teaspoon of cornstarch boiled in 250 mL of water, then cooled.
- **Malt water**: ground bikil (malted barley) soaked in warm water for 15 minutes, then strained.

1. Put single drops of iodine in a row on a white tile (about 12 drops).
2. Mix 10 mL of starch solution and 2 mL of malt water in a cup. **Start the timer.**
3. Every 30 seconds, take one drop of the mixture with a clean dropper and add it to the next iodine drop.
4. At first it turns **blue-black**. When the drop stays **yellow-brown**, all the starch has been broken down. Record that time.

Control: starch solution with 2 mL of **water** instead of malt water. Does it stay blue-black?

## Do: does temperature matter? (30 minutes)

Repeat the test with the starch and the malt water both warmed or cooled first, in water baths at:

- about 10 °C (ice bath),
- about 25 °C (room),
- about 40 °C,
- malt water that the guide has **boiled** for 2 minutes and cooled.

Each team does one or two temperatures, and the class shares results.

## Theory: enzymes

Read **Enzymes**. You'll learn:

- that enzymes are protein catalysts that speed up the reactions of life,
- how the lock-and-key model explains why each enzyme does only one job,
- why enzymes work faster when warm but stop when too hot,
- how to turn a time into a rate: $\text{rate} = 1/\text{time}$.

## Think

Some injera makers make **absit**: they take a cup of fermenting batter, cook it with boiling water until thick, and stir it back into the batter. Using what you've learned about enzymes and microbes, why might the cooked part be added back? (There's no single right answer: suggest two ideas.)

## Record

A table: temperature, time for starch to disappear, rate (1/time). A graph of rate against temperature. Explain the boiled-malt result using the word *denatured*.
