## Look, smell, listen

Your guide has four covered jars of teff batter, all made from the same flour and water:

- **Jar 1:** mixed just now.
- **Jar 2:** mixed yesterday.
- **Jar 3:** mixed two days ago.
- **Jar 4:** mixed three days ago.

For each jar, record:

- **Look:** colour, bubbles on top and inside, any layer of liquid (that's ersho), how high the batter is compared with the mark the guide made when it was mixed.
- **Smell:** waft the air towards you with your hand. Fresh? Sour? Like tella?
- **Listen:** hold your ear close to the open jar. Can you hear anything?

Then look at a finished injera. Count the eyes in a 2 cm × 2 cm square.

## Predict

On a page titled *The mystery*, write:

1. **What makes the bubbles?** Give your best guess and a second one.
2. **What makes the batter sour?**
3. **Why do injera makers keep a cup of ersho from the last batch?**
4. **What do you think would happen if the batter was kept in the fridge? Or in a hot place? Or if the flour was boiled first?**

## Questions

As a team, list every question you have. For example:

- Is something alive in there? Where did it come from?
- Why does it take three days, not one?
- Why do some injeras have more eyes than others?
- What's the absit (the cooked portion) for?
