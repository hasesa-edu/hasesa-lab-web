## Choose with data (25 minutes)

A builder wants mud that **shrinks little, doesn't crack, is strong, and resists rain**. No mix wins at everything, so score them.

For each measurement, rank the six mixes from 1 (best) to 6 (worst). Then add up each mix's ranks. The **lowest** total wins.

Do you think all five measurements matter equally? For an outside wall in the rains, maybe pit depth matters more. Agree a reason with your team before you change the scoring.

## Write your recommendation (15 minutes)

On a page titled *Our recommendation*, write:

1. **The mix:** soil, sand, straw and stabiliser in cups.
2. **The evidence:** at least three numbers from your tables that back it up (for example, "Mix D shrank 4% compared with 10% for soil alone").
3. **The weakness:** one thing your mix does badly, and how a builder could deal with it (for example a wide roof overhang, or a dung plaster coat).
4. **What you'd test next** to be more certain.

## Build: a chika panel (45 minutes)

Your guide has built small square frames from sticks, about 40 × 40 cm. You turn one into a piece of chika wall.

1. **Wattle.** Tie or nail thin sticks (mager) across the frame, every 5 cm, on both sides, like the rungs of a ladder. Ask the guide to help with nails.
2. **Mix** a big batch of your recommended mud: about four times the step 4 recipe. Let it rest while you finish the frame.
3. **Daub.** Throw handfuls of mud hard into the frame from one side, then the other, so it squeezes between the sticks and grips them. Fill it to about 3 cm thick.
4. **Smooth** the surface with wet hands or a flat stick. If you have dung, finish with a thin coat of sieved soil and dung mixed like thick paint.
5. Label the panel with your team and mix. It dries in the shade for a week; your guide will check it for cracks.

## Back to the mystery (5 minutes)

Open *The mystery* from step 1. Next to each guess write ✓ or ✗ and what you now know. Answer properly: **what was in the strong bar, and why did it work?** Use the words *clay, sand, straw, water, shrink, tension*.

## Explain it to the guide (15 minutes)

Your guide will ask each of you about your results. Be ready to:

- explain how clay holds mud together, and why too much clay cracks,
- show your jar test and its percentages,
- show your fair-test tables and graphs, and say what sand and straw each do,
- give your recommended mix and the evidence for it,
- show your panel and say how you'd protect a real chika wall from rain.
