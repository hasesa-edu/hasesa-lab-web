## Check your sausages (10 minutes)

Measure the three sausages from last time. Work out each one's shrinkage:

$$\text{shrinkage (\%)} = \frac{\text{wet length} - \text{dry length}}{\text{wet length}} \times 100$$

Which one shrank most? Which one cracked? Which one crumbles when you press it? Keep these numbers: they are a first clue.

## Theory: fair tests

Read **Fair tests and averages**. You'll learn:

- the independent, dependent and controlled variables,
- why you need at least three repeats,
- how to work out a mean and a range.

## The class plan (15 minutes)

The whole class tests **six mixes** together. Each team makes one or two of them. A "cup" is a 250 mL cup, filled loosely and levelled off.

| Mix | Soil | Sand | Chopped straw | Stabiliser |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 3 cups | none | none | none |
| B | 2 cups | 2 cups | none | none |
| C | 1½ cups | 3 cups | none | none |
| D | 2 cups | 2 cups | 1 cup | none |
| E | 2 cups | 2 cups | 2 cups | none |
| F | 2 cups | 2 cups | 1 cup | ½ cup ash, or 1 cup fresh dung |

Look at the table and answer in your notebook:

1. Which mixes would you compare to find out what **sand** does? (Hint: only the sand changes.)
2. Which mixes show what **straw** does?
3. Which two mixes show what the **stabiliser** does?
4. List at least four things everyone must keep the same.

**Water.** You can't add exactly the same water to every mix: sandy mixes need less. Instead, add water a little at a time until the mix passes the **drop test**: roll a ball 4 cm across and drop it from 1 m onto a hard floor. It should flatten but not burst into pieces. Record how much water you added.

## Build: mix and mould (50 minutes)

Gloves on. Mix outdoors or on a plastic sheet.

1. **Chop the straw** into pieces 2–3 cm long with scissors.
2. **Mix dry first.** Break up soil lumps and mix soil, sand (and ash) until the colour is even.
3. **Add water slowly**, mixing with your hands, until it passes the drop test. If you use dung, mix it with a little water first and add it now.
4. **Add the straw** last and knead until it is spread evenly.
5. **Mould.** Line each mould with a piece of plastic bag. Throw the mud in hard, so there are no air gaps, then press it down and smooth the top level with a ruler. For each mix make:
   - **3 bars** in the carton moulds (about 15 × 4 × 2 cm),
   - **5 small bricks** in matchbox trays.
6. **Mark the bars.** On top of each bar, press two toothpick holes exactly **100 mm** apart. You'll measure this distance again when the bar is dry.
7. **Label everything**: mix letter, team, number (A1, A2, A3…), with masking tape on the plastic.

## Measure before it dries (15 minutes)

Weigh **one bar of each mix** on the kitchen scale, in its mould, and weigh an empty lined mould. Record the mass of the wet mud.

## Drying (the guide does this)

Your bricks dry in the shade for about two weeks. After two days the guide takes them out of the moulds and turns them over so both sides dry. Strong sun dries the outside too fast and cracks it.

## Record

Write your fair-test plan in five lines (question, change, measure, keep the same, repeats). Predict: which mix will be strongest? Which will crack most? Which will survive rain best? Give a reason for each.
