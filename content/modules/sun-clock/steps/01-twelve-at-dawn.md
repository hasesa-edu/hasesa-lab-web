## The sun rules first (5 minutes)

Everything in this module is done with **shadows**. Copy these rules into the front of your notebook:

1. Never look at the sun. Not for a second, not through sunglasses, a tube, a phone camera or a reflection.
2. Hat on and water with you whenever you work outside. Wait in the shade between readings.
3. Sticks go in the ground, never waved around, with a cap on the top end.
4. Walk around the shadow plots, never across them.

## The puzzle (15 minutes)

Your guide writes two times on the board:

| | International (EAT) | Ethiopian |
|---|---|---|
| School starts | 8:00 am | 2 o'clock (ሁለት ሰዓት) |
| Lunch | 12:30 pm | 6:30 (ስድስት ተኩል) |

In pairs, work out:

1. How do you change Ethiopian time into international time? Write the rule.
2. What is Ethiopian **12 o'clock** in the morning? What happens at that moment, every day?
3. What would be the Ethiopian time at 9:00 pm? At midnight?

Now your guide shows you sunrise times for two cities:

| Date | Sunrise in Addis Ababa (EAT) | Sunrise in London (UK time) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 February | 6:46 am | 7:42 am |
| 15 June | 6:06 am | 4:43 am |
| 15 December | 6:32 am | 8:01 am |

Discuss: **why would a clock that starts at dawn work well in Addis, but badly in London?** Write your best guess. You'll come back to it in step 3.

## Watch and predict (20 minutes)

Your guide stands a 1 m stick upright in the sun.

On a page titled *The shadow mystery*, write and sketch your predictions:

1. Which way does the shadow point now? Which way will it point at noon? At 4 pm?
2. When will the shadow be **shortest**? Will it ever disappear completely?
3. Will the noon shadow be the same length tomorrow? In three months?
4. How could a shadow tell you which way is north, without a compass?

## Set up your stick (15 minutes)

Your team needs a level, sunny spot that stays out of the shade of buildings and trees from about 9 am to 3 pm.

1. Check the ground is level with a spirit level or a bottle half full of water.
2. Push your 1.00 m stick firmly into the ground, or stand it in a bucket of sand.
3. Hang the plumb line next to it. Adjust the stick until it is parallel to the string from two directions, at right angles to each other. It is now **vertical**.
4. Measure the height of the top above the ground. Write it down: it should be close to 1.00 m.
5. Mark the foot of the stick with chalk so you can put it back in exactly the same place.
