A spoon, a stick and a strip of rubber can throw a bean bag across a room. With the right numbers, they can throw it into a basin every time. In eight hours you build your own catapult, find the rules that decide where it lands, and then use those rules to hit a target you've never seen.

1. **The mystery shot** (45 min). Watch the guide hit a target first time. Learn the range rules and make your guesses.
2. **Build your catapult** (90 min). Frame, arm, pivot, rubber and cup, plus a pull-back scale so every shot is the same.
3. **Stretch and range** (75 min). How does pulling back further change how far it goes? Your first fair test and graph.
4. **The angle question** (75 min). Tilt your catapult and find the angle that throws furthest.
5. **How fast?** (75 min). Time the flight with a phone and work out the launch speed.
6. **Predict the landing** (60 min). Turn your graph into a calibration chart and test your predictions.
7. **Range day** (60 min). The guide places a target. You measure, calculate, and fire.

By the end you'll understand forces, energy stores, projectile motion, fair tests and graphs, and you'll have a catapult and a graph that together can hit a target on demand.
