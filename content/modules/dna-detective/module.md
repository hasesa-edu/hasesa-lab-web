DNA is the most famous molecule in science, but most people have never seen it. In twelve hours you'll see it, hold it, model it, follow it from parents to children, and use the same technique forensic scientists use to match a sample to a suspect.

1. **The invisible molecule** (45 min). Watch DNA appear out of a banana, and wonder how.
2. **Cells up close** (90 min). Look at plant and human cells and work out their real size.
3. **Break it open** (120 min). Extract DNA yourself, then test which steps really matter.
4. **Model the molecule** (90 min). Build a double helix and send a message in DNA code.
5. **Traits and chance** (120 min). Predict inheritance with Punnett squares, then test it.
6. **The gel** (150 min). Build an electrophoresis rig and separate a mock crime-scene sample.
7. **Case closed** (105 min). Solve the case and debate real-world DNA testing.

By the end you'll understand cells, DNA structure, base pairing, genes, inheritance, probability, enzymes and electrophoresis.
