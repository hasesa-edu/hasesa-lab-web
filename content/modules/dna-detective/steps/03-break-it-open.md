## Do: extract DNA (40 minutes)

Glasses and gloves on. Each team:

1. **Mash:** put half a ripe banana (or 3 strawberries) in a zip-lock bag. Squeeze out the air, seal it, and squash it with your fingers for 2 minutes, until there are no lumps.
2. **Extraction liquid:** in a cup, mix 100 mL of warm water, 1 teaspoon of washing-up liquid and ½ teaspoon of salt. Stir gently (don't make foam).
3. Add 2 tablespoons of the liquid to the bag. Squash gently for 1 more minute, without making bubbles.
4. **Protease (optional):** add a teaspoon of fresh pineapple juice or a pinch of meat tenderiser. Wait 5 minutes.
5. **Filter:** pour the mush through a coffee filter in a funnel into a clear tube, until it's about a quarter full.
6. **Alcohol (guide):** the guide tilts your tube and slowly pours ice-cold alcohol down the side until it forms a layer as deep as the fruit liquid. **Don't shake it.**
7. Wait 3 minutes. Watch the boundary. Twirl a skewer gently in the white strands, and lift them out.

## Theory: DNA and genes

Read **DNA and genes**. You'll learn the structure of DNA, what genes are, and what each step of the extraction does.

Then re-read the **protease** part of *Enzymes*. Why would breaking down proteins help get DNA out?

## Do: which steps matter? (50 minutes)

Each team runs the extraction again, leaving out **one** step, alongside a full method as a control. Teams choose different steps, so the class tests them all:

| Team | Leave out |
|---|---|
| 1 | the washing-up liquid |
| 2 | the salt |
| 3 | the pineapple/protease |
| 4 | use room-temperature alcohol instead of ice-cold |

Compare the amount of white DNA with the control: none, a little, the same, more. Photograph both tubes side by side.

## Notice something puzzling

The white clump looks big, but one DNA molecule is only 2 nm wide. Is everything in the clump DNA? What else might come along with it?

## Record (30 minutes)

A table of the class results. For each step, one sentence saying what it does, using your evidence. Update your guesses from *The mystery*: which were right?
