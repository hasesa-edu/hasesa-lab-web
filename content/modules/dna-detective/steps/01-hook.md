## Watch

Your guide mashes half a banana in a bag with a little water, washing-up liquid and salt, filters it into a tube, and then gently pours a layer of ice-cold alcohol on top.

Watch the line between the two layers. Within a minute or two, something white and cloudy starts to rise. The guide twirls a wooden skewer in it, and lifts out a sticky, stringy white clump.

That clump is **DNA**: millions of strands of it.

Look at it closely (don't touch). Write down what it looks like.

## Predict

On a page titled *The mystery*, write:

1. **Why do you think each ingredient was added?** The mashing, the washing-up liquid, the salt, the cold alcohol. Guess for each.
2. **Is the white clump only DNA, or could there be other things in it?**
3. **Would the same thing happen with an onion? With your own cheek cells? With a boiled banana?** Guess for each.
4. **Why does a banana even have DNA?**

## Questions

As a team, list every question you have. For example:

- If DNA is the instructions, what is it actually telling the cell to do?
- How do police use DNA to find criminals?
- Why do children look like their parents, but not exactly?
- How can so much information fit inside one cell?

## The first clue

DNA lives inside cells, behind a couple of layers of "skin". Read *Cells* tonight. Next time you'll look at cells yourself and work out how small they are.
