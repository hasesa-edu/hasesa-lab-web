Every day, sunlight pours enough energy onto a single roof to cook a family's meals. In eight hours you'll learn how heat moves, use that to build an oven that traps sunlight, and prove how well it works with your own measurements.

1. **The egg in the box** (45 min). See the oven work and make your predictions.
2. **Black, white and shiny** (60 min). Race cans of water in the sun and graph the results.
3. **Trap the heat** (75 min). Test lids and insulation to see how heat escapes.
4. **Build the oven** (120 min). A box in a box, a reflector, a window and a black pot.
5. **How much energy?** (75 min). Measure your oven's power and efficiency.
6. **Make it better** (60 min). Change one thing, test it fairly.
7. **Cook!** (60 min). Cook a real food and explain your oven.

By the end you'll understand conduction, convection, radiation, specific heat capacity, power and efficiency, and you'll have an oven that cooks with no fuel at all.
