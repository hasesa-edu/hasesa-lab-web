## Do: the cooling race (40 minutes)

This time you start **hot** and watch heat escape. The guide fills five paper cups with 150 mL of hot water (about 70 °C) from the same kettle. Each team takes one cup and sets it up:

| Cup | Setup |
|---|---|
| A | Nothing (the control) |
| B | A lid made from card |
| C | Wrapped in 3 layers of newspaper, no lid |
| D | Wrapped in newspaper, with a lid |
| E | Inside a bigger cup with crumpled paper packed between them, and a lid |

Measure the temperature every 3 minutes for 24 minutes. Everyone takes readings at the same time.

## Notice something puzzling

The cup with only a lid may cool more slowly than the cup with only newspaper. Why would a thin card lid make so much difference? Where is most of the heat escaping from an open cup?

Hold your hand (carefully) above an open cup and beside it. Where do you feel the most warmth?

## Theory

Re-read the **convection** and **conduction** parts of *Heat transfer*. Then decide which cup stops which kind of heat loss.

## Do: the greenhouse (20 minutes)

Two identical black cans of cold water in the sun. Cover one with a clear plastic bag tied around it (with air trapped inside), and leave the other open. Measure both after 15 minutes.

## Record

Cooling graph of all five cups. For each cup: total temperature drop, and which heat losses it reduced. Then plan your oven: what will go **between** your two boxes, and what will go on **top**?
