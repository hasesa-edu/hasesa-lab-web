## Cook (set up at the start of the day)

With your guide, choose something to cook: an egg in a little water, rice, or small pieces of potato. Set your oven up in the morning sun, aimed and sealed, with the food and its water in the black pot. Re-aim it every 20–30 minutes.

When the guide checks it with oven gloves, it must be **hot all the way through**. Eat it straight away, or don't eat it.

## Back to the mystery (15 minutes)

Open *The mystery* from step 1. Next to each guess write ✓ or ✗ and what you now know. Then answer the first question properly: **how did a cardboard box get hot enough to cook?** Use the words *radiation, absorb, reflect, convection, conduction, insulation*.

## Explain it to the guide (30 minutes)

Your guide will ask each of you to:

- point to the parts of your oven that deal with radiation, convection and conduction, and say what each does,
- show your temperature graph and heating rate,
- show your energy, power and efficiency calculations,
- explain your fair test and what it showed,
- say whether a family could cook with this every day in your area, and what would make it hard (season, clouds, time).

Take your oven home. On the next sunny weekend, try heating water for tea with it.
