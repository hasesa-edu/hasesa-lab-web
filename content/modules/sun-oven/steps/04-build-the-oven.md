## The design

Your oven is a **box inside a box**:

- the **inner box** holds the pot; it's lined with foil and has a black floor,
- the **gap** between the boxes is packed with insulation,
- a **clear window** seals the top of the inner box,
- a **reflector flap**, covered in foil, bounces extra sunlight in.

Draw your plan with measurements before cutting.

## Build (90 minutes)

1. **Base.** Put 3–5 cm of crumpled newspaper in the bottom of the big box. Stand the small box on it, in the middle.
2. **Insulate.** Pack crumpled newspaper, cloth or straw firmly into the gap all the way round. Not too tight: trapped air does the insulating.
3. **Collar.** Cut card strips to cover the gap at the top, so insulation can't fall in. Tape them down.
4. **Line.** Glue foil, shiny side out, over the inside walls of the inner box. Smooth it flat; wrinkles scatter light.
5. **Floor.** Put a sheet of black card, or paint the floor matt black.
6. **Window.** Tape clear plastic (stretched tight, two layers with a gap is better) or the guide's taped-edge glass over the top of the inner box. It must seal: no gaps for warm air.
7. **Reflector.** Cut a lid flap from card as wide as the box. Cover it with foil and hinge it along the back edge with tape. Prop it with a stick.

## Aim it (10 minutes)

Take the oven into the sun. Turn it until the box casts the **smallest shadow** behind it. Adjust the reflector until the sunlight it bounces falls on the window. **Look at shadows, not at the sun or its reflection.**

## First test (20 minutes)

Put 0.5 kg (500 mL) of water in the black pot with the lid on and a thermometer through a hole in the lid. Seal the oven. Record the temperature every 5 minutes. Re-aim the oven every 20 minutes as the sun moves.

## Record

A labelled photo or drawing of your oven, the temperature readings, and **one thing** that went wrong or could be better.
