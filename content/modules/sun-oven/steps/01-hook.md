## Look

Your guide's solar oven has been sitting in the sun since the start of the day. Put on oven gloves before touching anything. Let the guide open the lid, away from faces, and take out the pot.

- Feel the air above the open box with your hand (don't touch the pot).
- The guide reads the thermometer in the pot. What does it say?
- The guide peels the egg. Is it cooked?

Then look at the oven carefully. Write down every part you can see, and what each part is made of.

## Predict

On a page titled *The mystery*, write:

1. **How did a cardboard box get hot enough to cook?** Write your best guess and a second one.
2. **Why is the pot black?** What would happen with a shiny pot?
3. **What do you think the shiny flap is for?** And the clear window?
4. **Guess:** how hot would water in an open pot, sitting in the sun with no box, get?

## Test one prediction (10 minutes)

The guide puts a black pot of water out in the sun with no box, and a thermometer in it. You'll check it at the end of the session. Was your guess for question 4 right?

## Questions

As a team, list every question you have. For example:

- Does it work on a cloudy day? In the rainy season?
- Could it boil water?
- Why doesn't the cardboard catch fire?
- Could a family really cook every day with one?
