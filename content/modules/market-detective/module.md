Every week, people in Addis Ababa buy honey, berbere, milk and kibe at the market. Most traders are honest. But a few stretch their goods with cheap fillers: sugar syrup in honey, water and flour in milk, brick dust and dye in berbere, mashed potato in butter. Fakes cheat buyers, and some, like illegal red dyes or grit, can harm health.

In this module you are the forensic team. You open a case file of sixteen samples, look closely, make a plan, and then run real tests: weighing and measuring density, the iodine test for starch, paper chromatography for dyes, a homemade pH indicator, and sedimentation for grit. Each test pulls in just the theory you need to understand what you see and to calculate with it.

By the end you will name every fake, explain exactly how it was faked, and prove it with evidence a judge would accept.
