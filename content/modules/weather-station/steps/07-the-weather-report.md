## Prepare (40 minutes)

Each team prepares a three-minute weather report, like a TV forecaster, but with your own data:

1. **The station:** show one instrument and how it works.
2. **The week:** show your lined-up graphs and the most interesting thing they show.
3. **The forecast:** your rule, the scoreboard, and an honest verdict: did it beat persistence?
4. **Tomorrow:** your forecast for tomorrow, using your rule.

## Present (30 minutes)

Give your report to the class. Other teams ask one question each.

## Back to the mystery (10 minutes)

Open *The mystery* from step 1. Was your first forecast right? Can a class with home-made instruments predict the weather better than a guess? Answer honestly, with numbers.

## Explain it to the guide (10 minutes)

Your guide will ask each of you to:

- explain how one instrument works and how it was calibrated,
- show a rainfall calculation,
- point out a pattern in the graphs,
- explain the scoreboard and what it shows,
- say what you'd improve to make better forecasts.

Keep the station running if you can. A year of data from your school would be valuable for everyone.
