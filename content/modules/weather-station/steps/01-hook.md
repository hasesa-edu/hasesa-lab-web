## Look outside (10 minutes)

Go outside together. Without any instruments, write down everything you can about the weather right now:

- the sky: clear, a few clouds, covered? What kinds of clouds?
- the wind: none, gentle, strong? Which way is it blowing from? How can you tell?
- the temperature: hot, warm, cool? Is it the same in the sun and the shade?
- the air: dry or damp? Any dew on the grass?

## Predict

On a page titled *The mystery*, write a forecast for **tomorrow at the same time**: rain or no rain, cloudy or clear, windy or calm. Write **why** you think so.

Then write the forecast from a phone weather app, if someone has one, and the answer to "tomorrow will be like today". Tomorrow, you'll check all three.

## How do people forecast?

Talk as a class:

- Ask someone at home how farmers in your family's area know the rains are coming. Write down any signs they use (birds, winds, clouds, the moon, the feel of the air).
- How do phone forecasts work? Where do their numbers come from?

## Questions

As a team, list every question you have. For example:

- What actually makes it rain?
- Why is kiremt so regular, but belg so unreliable?
- Can you measure "how windy"?
- Why do the nights in bega get so cold?

## The first clue

Read *Air and weather* tonight. Pay attention to the five things a weather station measures. You'll build an instrument for each one.
