Weather affects everything in Ethiopia, from the harvest to the journey to school. In twelve hours you'll build the same kinds of instruments meteorologists use, run a real weather station for at least a week, and see whether your data can beat a simple guess.

1. **Can we predict it?** (45 min). How do people forecast? Make your first prediction.
2. **The rain gauge** (75 min). Build one and learn to turn millilitres into millimetres of rain.
3. **Air has weight** (90 min). Crush a can with air, then build a barometer.
4. **Wind and wet air** (120 min). Build an anemometer, a wind vane and a hygrometer. Calibrate them.
5. **A week of data** (150 min). Run the station: readings every day, at the same time.
6. **The forecast** (90 min). Find patterns, make forecasts, and keep score.
7. **The weather report** (90 min). Present your station, your data and your score.

Step 5 runs over at least a week: a few minutes of readings every school day, plus the session time to graph and discuss.

By the end you'll understand air pressure, humidity, rainfall, wind speed, calibration, graphing over time and testing a prediction honestly.
