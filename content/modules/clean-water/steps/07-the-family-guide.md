## Run the whole chain (25 minutes)

Start with 1 L of freshly stirred muddy water. Put it through your full chain:

1. **Settle** with your best coagulant from step 3.
2. **Filter** through your filter from step 4.
3. **Disinfect** with the method your team chooses (the guide does any boiling or chlorine).
4. **Store** in a clean covered bottle.

Measure clarity at each stage. Run the yeast test on a separate batch of yeast water treated with your disinfection stage, if you haven't already.

Remember: **nobody drinks it**. Our tests show how each stage works; a real safety test needs a laboratory.

## Back to the mystery (10 minutes)

Open your page *The mystery*. Answer the first question properly: **how can clear water still make you sick?** Use the words *microbe, size, filter, disinfect*.

## Make the family guide (25 minutes)

Make a one-page guide, with pictures, for a family that collects water from a river and has no electricity. It should say:

- the steps in order, and **why** each one matters,
- what to do if there's no fuel, and what to do on cloudy days,
- how to store water so it stays safe,
- one mistake people often make, and how to avoid it.

Your guide will ask you to explain your page, your clarity results and your yeast results.
