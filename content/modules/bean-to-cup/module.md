An Ethiopian coffee ceremony is a chemistry lab: beans roasted over charcoal, ground by hand, boiled in a jebena and poured three times. In eight hours you'll take it apart, measure every stage, and find out why small changes make such a big difference to the cup.

1. **Three cups** (45 min). Smell, look and (if you like) taste. What's different?
2. **Roast it** (90 min). Follow a roast from green to dark, weighing and timing as it goes.
3. **What dissolves?** (75 min). Dissolving, concentration, and a colour scale to measure strength.
4. **Grind and extract** (90 min). Fine vs coarse, short vs long: graph how much dissolves.
5. **Sour or bitter?** (60 min). Measure the acidity of different roasts.
6. **Your recipe** (60 min). Design the best cup and prove it with data.

By the end you'll understand rates of reaction, surface area, solutions and concentration, dilution, heat transfer and pH, all through one cup of coffee.
