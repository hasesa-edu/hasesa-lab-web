## Look and smell

Your guide brewed three cups of coffee, labelled X, Y and Z, from the **same** beans and the **same** water. Let them cool a little first.

For each cup, record:

- **Colour:** hold it against white paper. Light brown? Dark? Can you see the bottom?
- **Smell:** waft it towards you. Fruity? Toasty? Burnt?
- **Clarity:** cloudy or clear? Any grounds?
- **Taste (optional, one small sip each):** sour? bitter? sweet? strong? watery?

Then look at the beans and grounds the guide used for each cup. Compare their colour and how fine they are.

## Predict

On a page titled *The mystery*, write:

1. **Why do X, Y and Z taste different?** Give your best two guesses.
2. **What do you think happens to a coffee bean when it's roasted?** It goes in green and comes out brown. What changes?
3. **Why do you think coffee is ground before brewing?** What would happen with whole beans?
4. **What actually ends up in the cup?** Is it the bean, or something from the bean?

Your guide will tell you how each cup was made. How many of your guesses were right?

## Questions

As a team, list every question you have. For example:

- What makes coffee bitter? What makes it sour?
- Why does the smell change when beans are roasted?
- Why is the second and third pour of the ceremony (tona, baraka) weaker?
- How could we measure how "strong" a cup is, without tasting?
