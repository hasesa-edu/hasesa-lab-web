## Design (20 minutes)

As a team, write a brewing recipe for the cup you think is best. It must say:

- **Roast:** light, medium or dark, and how you'll know when to stop roasting.
- **Grind:** fine or coarse.
- **Ratio:** grams of coffee per 100 mL of water.
- **Water temperature.**
- **Brewing time.**

For every choice, write one sentence of **evidence** from your own data: "We chose a fine grind for 4 minutes because our graph shows it reaches 90% of its maximum strength by then, and longer brewing added little."

## Brew and test (20 minutes)

The guide brews your recipe. Measure its strength with your colour scale and its pH. Do they match what you predicted?

## Back to the mystery (10 minutes)

Open *The mystery* from step 1. Mark each guess ✓ or ✗. Then explain cups X, Y and Z: which was under-extracted (too little dissolved), which over-extracted, and which balanced? Use the words *roast, surface area, time, temperature, dissolve, acidic*.

## Explain it to the guide (10 minutes)

Your guide will ask each of you about your roast data, your colour scale, your extraction graph, your pH results and your recipe. Be ready to name the four factors that change a rate, with an example of each from your own measurements.
