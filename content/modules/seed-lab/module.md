Farmers, gardeners and scientists argue about what makes plants grow. In this module you stop arguing and start testing. Over about two weeks you'll design your own experiment, grow it, measure it, and prove your answer, the way real plant scientists do.

1. **Two seeds** (45 min). Why does one seed grow and another not? Make predictions.
2. **Inside a seed** (60 min). Dissect a soaked seed, find the baby plant and test its food store.
3. **Plan a fair test** (90 min). Choose your question and plan an experiment that really answers it.
4. **Plant your experiment** (90 min). Build your growing station and plant it. Then water and measure every few days.
5. **Light and leaves** (90 min). Test a leaf for starch and discover what light does for a plant.
6. **Measure and graph** (120 min). Two weeks later: final measurements, means, graphs and growth rates.
7. **The poster** (75 min). Present your evidence like a scientist.

Between steps 4 and 6, you'll need about 5 minutes every two days to water and measure.

By the end you'll understand germination, photosynthesis, cells and limiting factors, and how to design an experiment whose answer you can trust.
