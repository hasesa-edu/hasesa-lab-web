## Final measurements (25 minutes)

About two weeks after planting, take your final measurements of every pot, exactly the same way as before. Then, for extra evidence:

- Count the leaves on each plant.
- Note the colour (dark green, pale green, yellow).
- With the guide, gently tip out one plant from each group, wash the soil off the roots, and lay them side by side. Photograph them on a ruler.

## Calculate (30 minutes)

For each group, on each measuring day:

1. The **mean** height (count plants that didn't germinate as 0, or leave them out and say so: decide as a team and be consistent).
2. The **range**.
3. The **germination percentage**.

## Theory: graphs

Read **Graphs and relationships**. You'll learn which variable goes on which axis, how to draw a best-fit line or curve, how to calculate a gradient, and what the shape of a graph tells you.

## Graph (35 minutes)

Draw **one graph** with time (days) on the x-axis and mean height on the y-axis. One line per group, each in a different colour, with a key.

Then calculate the **growth rate** (cm per day) for each group over the fastest-growing part of the graph.

If your question was about germination, also draw a bar chart of germination % for each group.

## Think (30 minutes)

Answer these in your notebook, as a team:

1. What does your graph show? Describe the difference between the groups, with numbers.
2. Is the difference between groups **bigger than the spread** within each group? How sure are you?
3. Did anything go wrong? Did any outliers have a known cause?
4. What would you do differently if you ran it again?
5. Compare with another team that tested something else. Do your results fit together? (For example, the light team and the water team.)
