## Look

Your guide planted chickpeas two weeks ago, in several pots, and treated each pot a little differently. You don't know how. Look at each pot carefully:

- Which seeds came up? Which didn't?
- Are the plants the same height? The same colour? Are some tall and thin, some short and bushy?
- Gently look at the roots of one plant in each pot, if the guide lets you.

Write down everything you notice about each pot. Draw them.

## Predict

On a page titled *The mystery*, for each pot write your guess: **what did the guide do differently?** Give a reason based on what you see.

Then write:

1. **What do you think a seed needs to start growing?** List everything.
2. **Does a seed need light to start? Does it need soil?** Write yes or no, and why.
3. **Where does a plant's mass come from?** It started as a small seed and became a plant many times heavier. Where did the extra stuff come from?

Your guide will reveal what happened to each pot. How many did you get right?

## Opinions vs evidence

Here are some things people say about plants. Some might be true, some false:

- "Plants grow better if you talk to them."
- "Seeds need soil to germinate."
- "A little salt in the water helps plants grow."
- "Seeds planted close together all grow better."
- "Plants grow towards light."

As a team, choose two that you'd like to test. Next time, you'll learn how to test one properly.

## The first clue

Read *Photosynthesis and plant growth* tonight. Was your answer to question 3 right?
