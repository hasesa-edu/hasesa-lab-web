## Build your growing station (30 minutes)

1. Make drainage holes in every cup (the guide uses a hot nail).
2. Fill every pot with the **same amount** of the same soil, pressed down the same way. Use a cup measure.
3. Label every pot with your team name, group and number (e.g. *T3 – Salt 2 g – #4*).
4. Set up anything special your test needs: a dark box with no gaps, a shade cover, salt solutions mixed and labelled, measured water in bottles.

If you're testing germination in bags, fold a wet paper towel into each clear bag, put the seeds along a line drawn 3 cm from the top, and tape the bags to a window or wall.

## Plant (20 minutes)

- Choose seeds that look similar in size. Throw out any cracked or tiny ones.
- Soak all seeds for the same time (or none at all), if your plan says so.
- Plant every seed at the same depth: about twice its own thickness. Use a pencil mark.
- Water every pot with **exactly** the amount in your plan, using a syringe or measuring jug.

## Day 0 record (15 minutes)

Fill in your results table for Day 0. Take a photo of your whole setup.

## The care plan (25 minutes)

Your experiment runs for about two weeks. Agree as a team:

- who comes to water and measure on which days (about 5 minutes, every two days),
- exactly how much water each pot gets and when,
- what to do if something goes wrong (a pot falls, an insect eats a seedling): **record it**, don't hide it.

Write the rota in your notebook and on a card next to your pots.

## Between sessions

Every two days: water as planned, and measure. Record **every** pot, even the ones that haven't come up yet (write 0 or "not germinated"). Note anything odd. Take a photo each time.
