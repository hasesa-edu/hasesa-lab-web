You start with a mystery: a radio that plays with no battery. Over ten hours you find out how it works by building your own, one part at a time.

1. **The mystery** (45 min). Listen to the guide's crystal radio. Make predictions, look at the parts, and write down your questions.
2. **Make waves** (75 min). Send waves along ropes, slinkies and water. Measure them, and find the rule $v = f\lambda$.
3. **Coil speaker** (90 min). Wind a coil, add a magnet and a paper cup, and play music from your phone. Electricity plus magnetism makes motion.
4. **Antenna** (90 min). String up a long wire and a ground. Work out how long a real station's radio wave is.
5. **Tune in** (120 min). Wind a tuning coil and add a capacitor. Discover resonance and pick out one station.
6. **The detector** (90 min). Add a diode and hear sound for the first time. Find out why one-way current turns radio into sound.
7. **Finish** (90 min). Mount it all on a board, log the stations you can hear, and explain the whole chain in your own words.

By the end you'll understand waves, frequency, electromagnetism, the electromagnetic spectrum, resonance, diodes and AM. You'll have solved real problems with them, and you'll have a radio that runs on energy carried through the air from a transmitter.
