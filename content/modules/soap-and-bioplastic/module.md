Plastics and soaps are some of the most-used chemicals on Earth. In twelve hours you'll make both from ingredients sold in any market, work out the chemistry behind them, and test whether your products are any good.

1. **From the market** (45 min). Soap from oil, plastic from starch? Predict how.
2. **Oil and water** (75 min). Why oil and water won't mix, and what soap does about it.
3. **Starch to plastic** (120 min). Cook a bioplastic, and test how a plasticiser changes it.
4. **Calculate the lye** (75 min). Use SAP values to design a safe soap recipe.
5. **Make soap** (150 min). Make cold-process soap with your guide, following strict safety rules.
6. **Test and compare** (120 min). Weeks later: test your soap and your plastic against shop-bought ones.
7. **The product pitch** (90 min). Present your products and the chemistry behind them.

Step 6 happens about four weeks after step 5, while the soap cures.

By the end you'll understand polymers, plasticisers, fats and oils, saponification, reacting quantities, pH and biodegradation.
