## Look and handle

Your guide shows you:

- a bar of home-made soap (cured for weeks, safe to touch), next to a bottle of cooking oil,
- a clear, bendy sheet of bioplastic, next to a bag of starch,
- a shop-bought soap and a supermarket plastic bag.

Handle the soaps and plastics (not the raw ingredients). For each pair, write down how the product differs from its ingredient: feel, look, smell, whether it dissolves in water, whether it bends or snaps.

Drop a little oil into a glass of water. Drop a pinch of starch into another. What happens?

## Predict

On a page titled *The mystery*, write:

1. **How can oil become soap?** Something must be added or done to it. Guess what.
2. **How can a powder become a bendy sheet?**
3. **Why do you think some plastics rot away in soil and others don't?**
4. **What do you think "chemical reaction" means here?** Is making soap like melting butter, or like cooking an egg?

## Questions

As a team, list every question you have. For example:

- How does soap actually remove dirt?
- Is home-made soap safe?
- Could bioplastic replace plastic bags?
- Why did people make soap from ash in the past?
