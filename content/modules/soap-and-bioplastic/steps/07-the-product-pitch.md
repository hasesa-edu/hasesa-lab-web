## Prepare your pitch (35 minutes)

Imagine you're starting a small business making soap or bioplastic in your area. Prepare a three-minute pitch for one product:

- **What it is** and what it's for.
- **How it's made:** the chemistry, in simple words, with the word equation (for soap) or the role of each ingredient (for bioplastic).
- **The evidence:** your test results against the shop-bought product.
- **Safety:** what makes it safe to make and to use.
- **The cost:** estimate the cost of ingredients per bar or per sheet from market prices.
- **Why it's better** for people or the environment, and its honest weakness.

## Pitch (30 minutes)

Each team pitches to the class. The audience asks one question each and votes for the product they'd buy, giving a reason.

## Back to the mystery (10 minutes)

Open *The mystery* from step 1. Next to each guess, write ✓ or ✗ and what you now know. Then answer properly: **how do oil and starch turn into something completely different?** Use the words *polymer, plasticiser, saponification, glycerol, chemical reaction*.

## Explain it to the guide (15 minutes)

Your guide will ask each of you to:

- explain what the heating, vinegar and glycerol do in the bioplastic, with your graph,
- show and explain your lye calculation, including the superfat,
- write the saponification word equation and explain how soap removes grease,
- give your soap's pH and say why it matters,
- compare the biodegradation of your bioplastic and the plastic bag, with numbers.

Your soap is yours to take home, once its pH has been checked.
