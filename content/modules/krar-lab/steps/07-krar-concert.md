## Retune and practise (15 minutes)

Your strings have stretched since last time. Glasses on, and check every string with the tuner again. Write down how far each one had drifted.

Practise a short tune. Number your strings 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest). Start with this pattern, then try a melody you know in your qenet:

**1 2 3 5 · 3 2 1 · 1 2 3 4 5 · 4 3 2 1**

## Back to the mystery (10 minutes)

Open your page titled *The singing line* from step 1. Next to each guess, write ✓ or ✗ and what you now know. Then answer properly:

1. What makes one note higher than another? Use the words *frequency*, *length*, *tension* and *thickness*.
2. What does the box do? Use the word *resonance*.
3. If you halve the length, what happens to the note? If you hang four times the weight?

## Concert and explanation (20 minutes)

Each team plays its tune for the group. Then your guide asks each of you about your work. Be ready to:

- show your graph of $f$ against $1/L$ and say what the straight line means,
- show your tension and thickness tables and state the pattern for each,
- explain your sound-box results with numbers,
- show how you worked out your five target frequencies from the ratios,
- explain why azmaris retune their krar between songs.

Take your krar home. Keep retuning it: the nylon will keep stretching for a few days.
