## What a monochord is (5 minutes)

A monochord ("one string") is a measuring instrument. Pythagoras used one in ancient Greece to study musical notes. It lets you change **one thing at a time**: the length, the weight pulling the string, or the string itself. That makes fair tests possible, which a real krar can't do.

## Build (40 minutes)

You need a plank about 80 cm long, a 0.5 mm nylon line, two round pencils, a thicker round pen, a 5 cm piece of smooth plastic pipe, tape and a 1.5 L bottle.

1. **The anchor.** Your guide has put a screw into one end of the plank. Tie the line to it with a double knot.
2. **The fixed bridge.** Tape a pencil across the plank 5 cm from the screw. The line runs over it.
3. **The far bridge.** Tape the plastic pipe across the other end of the plank, right on the edge. The line runs over the pipe and hangs down.
4. **Measure.** Mark the plank every 5 cm, starting from the fixed bridge. The **vibrating length** is the distance between the two bridges.
5. **The weight.** Lay the plank on the table with the pipe end sticking out 10 cm past the edge. Tape or clamp the plank so it can't slide. Fill the 1.5 L bottle with water, tie the line tightly around its neck, and **slowly** let it hang. Put the catch-bucket under it, no more than 10 cm below.
6. **The movable bridge.** Keep the thicker pen ready. You will slide it under the line to make the vibrating part shorter.

Glasses on before the bottle hangs.

## First note (15 minutes)

Pluck the line in the middle with your fingertip. Open the tuner app on a phone lying on the plank, close to the line.

1. The app shows a number in **Hz** (hertz) and a note name. Write both down.
2. Pluck three times and read it each time. Do you get the same number? Write all three.
3. Pluck gently, then hard. Does the number change? Does the loudness change?

Hertz means **vibrations per second**. If the app says 260 Hz, the line goes back and forth 260 times every second.

## Check it works (10 minutes)

A good monochord gives the same reading, within a few hertz, every time you pluck. If yours doesn't:

- Is the bottle swinging? Wait until it is still.
- Is the line slipping on the anchor? Retie it.
- Is the line rubbing on the table edge? Move the plank out further.
- Nylon stretches when you first load it. Leave it hanging for two minutes and measure again.

## Record (5 minutes)

Draw your monochord and label: anchor, fixed bridge, far bridge, vibrating length, weight. Write down your vibrating length, the bottle's mass, and your three frequency readings.
